📋 Vendor Policies Every Applicant Should Know ▾
💵
Booth pricing isn't always on the event page. Some sites (like drinkeatrelax.com) publish it in a separate vendor application PDF; others (like eventeny.com) show it directly.
📝
Deposits: Typically 50% is due when you submit your application, with the balance due before the event.
🕐
Setup windows: Usually the day before or early morning of the event — always confirm in the event's vendor handbook.
📍
Booth assignment: You can often request a location, but assignments are usually finalized by show management, not guaranteed in advance.
🚫
No sharing or reselling your booth space with another vendor — each vendor typically must apply individually.
General patterns compiled from several event/vendor sites. Always confirm current details directly with the organizer before applying.
