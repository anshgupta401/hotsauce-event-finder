📋 Vendor Policies Every Applicant Should Know ▾
💵
Booth pricing isn't on the event page. Each event series publishes its own vendor application PDF on der411.com with exact booth prices (e.g. 10'x10' spaces typically range $350–$895 depending on category).
📝
Deposits: Typically 50% is due when you submit your application, with the balance due before the event.
🕐
Setup windows: Usually Friday 12–5pm and Saturday 8–11am, though this varies by venue — always confirm in your Event Manual.
📍
Booth assignment: You can request a location, but assignments are finalized by show management and given out on arrival — not guaranteed in advance.
🪑
Tables: Alcohol vendors are typically supplied one 6ft table; food vendors often are not — bring your own if unsure.
🔌
Electricity is usually a separate add-on, not included in the base booth price.
🚫
No sharing or reselling your booth space with another vendor — each vendor must apply individually.
Sourced from der411.com's vendor FAQ and application terms. Always confirm current details with sales@drinkeatrelax.com before applying, since policies can change per season.
